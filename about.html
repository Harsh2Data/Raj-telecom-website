Our story

Started on one simple idea: fix it right, explain it plainly.

Raj Telecom began as a single repair counter, built on a habit that stuck — never touch a phone without telling its owner exactly what's wrong and what it'll cost.

Most people don't walk into a repair shop feeling confident. They're worried about the cost, worried the shop will find "extra problems," worried they won't understand what's actually being fixed. Raj Telecom exists to remove that worry.

Every phone that comes through the door gets a ticket, a clear diagnosis, and a price you approve before any work starts. It's a small thing, but it's the reason customers come back — and send their friends and family to us instead of explaining the same problem to a stranger.

What we stand for

A few things we don't compromise on.

Honesty over upselling

If your phone doesn't need a part replaced, we won't sell you one. We tell you the real issue, every time.

Respect for your time

Most repairs happen while you wait. We'd rather you leave with a working phone than a promise to "come back later."

Standing behind the work

Every repair carries a warranty. If something's wrong because of our work, we fix it again — no arguments.
