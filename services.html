What we fix

Every part of the phone, covered.

Whatever the issue, we diagnose it properly first and explain it in plain words before any work begins.

Broken Display

Cracked glass or a display that won't show anything right.

Touch

Screen doesn't respond, or responds in the wrong place.

Charging

Won't charge, charges slowly, or drains while plugged in.

Water Damage

Dropped in water — bring it in before switching it on.

Lost Data

Photos, contacts, or files that suddenly disappeared.

Sound

Speaker crackling, muffled, or no sound at all.

Charging Jack

Loose connection or a port that won't hold the cable.

Sensor

Proximity, fingerprint, or face-unlock not responding.

Broken Panel

Cracked or dented back panel or frame.

Aux Jack

Headphone jack loose, silent, or only working one side.

Camera

Blurry photos, black screen, or a cracked lens.

Mic

People can't hear you clearly on calls or recordings.
