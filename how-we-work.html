Common questions

Things people usually ask before their first visit.

Is the diagnosis free?+
Yes. We check the device and give you a clear price before any repair work begins — you only pay if you go ahead.
How long does a typical repair take?+
Most screen, battery, and charging port repairs are done within 30–90 minutes. Water damage or complex board issues may take longer, and we'll tell you upfront.
Do you use genuine parts?+
Yes — every part we use is sourced from verified suppliers and tested before installation. We'll always tell you the part grade being used.
What does the warranty actually cover?+
Parts and labour on the specific repair are covered for 6 months against manufacturing or fitting faults — not against new physical damage.
Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?+
Both work. Booking a slot just means a technician is free the moment you arrive, so there's no wait.
